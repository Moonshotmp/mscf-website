1. Acknowledgment & Assumption of Risk — Including the Risks of Unsupervised Training

I understand that fitness training at Moonshot CrossFit, located at 542 Busse Hwy, Park Ridge, IL 60068, including the interior premises, the parking lot, sidewalks adjacent to the premises, and any common areas under Moonshot CrossFit's control (collectively, the "Facility"), involves inherent and significant risks, including risks arising from the negligence of the Released Parties. I voluntarily, knowingly, and expressly assume full responsibility for all such risks, whether known or unknown.

The activities I may engage in include weightlifting, Olympic-style barbell movements (snatch, clean and jerk, deadlift, squat, press), gymnastics movements (pull-ups, muscle-ups, ring movements, handstand work, rope climbs), plyometrics (box jumps, broad jumps), high-intensity interval training, conditioning, running, rowing, cycling, and any other self-directed exercise (collectively, "Activities").

The risks I assume include, without limitation:
• Physiological injury — muscle strains, sprains, tears, fractures, joint dislocations, head injury, concussion, dental injury, eye injury, heart attack, sudden cardiac arrest, stroke, heat illness, dehydration, rhabdomyolysis, paralysis, and death;
• Movement-specific catastrophic injury — dropped or rebounding barbells (including bar-on-neck, bar-on-chest, and bar-on-foot), failed lifts, missed reps, falls from pull-up rigs, gymnastics rings, ropes, and boxes;
• Equipment failure — including but not limited to barbells, collars, racks, rigs, plyo boxes, resistance bands, ropes, rings, pulleys, flooring, and HVAC equipment;
• Premises conditions — slip, trip, or fall on chalk, sweat, water, ice, snow, uneven surfaces, parking-lot conditions, or inadequate lighting;
• Emergency-response risks specific to unsupervised hours — delayed or absent emergency response; absence of a coach, trainer, staff member, or other supervisor available to demonstrate movements, correct form, spot lifts, perform CPR, retrieve or operate the AED, or call 911 on my behalf; injury or death occurring without witnesses; lone-training emergencies;
• Acts of third parties — including criminal acts such as assault, battery, sexual assault, robbery, theft, and unauthorized entry by persons admitted in violation of Facility rules, as well as the negligent or reckless acts of other members training simultaneously;
• Facility-system risks — power outage, HVAC failure causing extreme temperature exposure, access-control failure, and ingress/egress conditions during late-night and early-morning hours.

I acknowledge that no person is on duty at the Facility during fob hours to retrieve the AED, perform CPR, summon medical assistance, intervene in a third-party criminal act, or otherwise respond to emergencies in real time. I agree to carry a charged mobile telephone capable of calling 911 while training during fob hours.

Moonshot CrossFit is not responsible for loss, damage, or theft of my personal property, including items in lockers, items left at the Facility, and vehicles or items in vehicles in the parking lot.

2. 24/7 Self-Directed Use

I understand that 24/7 key fob access provides entry to the Facility outside of coached class hours, when no coach, trainer, staff member, or other supervisor will be present. I acknowledge that I am training entirely on my own. I represent that I am physically capable of engaging in unsupervised exercise, that I have either consulted a physician or freely chosen not to do so (subject to §3 below), and that I assume all risks associated with self-directed training without supervision.

3. Medical Representation & Physical Activity Readiness

I represent that I am in good physical condition and have no medical condition (cardiovascular, musculoskeletal, neurological, or otherwise) that would prevent me from engaging in strenuous, unsupervised exercise. Specifically, I represent that as of today: (1) no doctor has told me that I have a heart condition or high blood pressure; (2) I do not feel pain in my chest at rest, during my daily activities, or when I do physical activity; (3) I do not lose balance because of dizziness and I have not lost consciousness in the last 12 months; (4) I have not been diagnosed with another chronic medical condition (other than heart disease or high blood pressure) that makes exercise unsafe for me; (5) I am not taking prescribed medication for a chronic medical condition, or my physician has cleared me to exercise while taking it; (6) I do not currently have, and have not had within the past 12 months, a bone, joint, or soft-tissue problem that could be made worse by becoming more physically active; and (7) no doctor has ever said that I should only do medically supervised physical activity.

If I cannot truthfully make every representation above, I will not sign this Agreement until I have obtained written physician clearance for unsupervised exercise and provided it to Moonshot CrossFit. If my condition changes, I will notify Moonshot CrossFit and discontinue training as appropriate. I agree to seek immediate medical attention for any injury or illness I experience.

6. Fob Use & Facility Rules

I acknowledge and agree to the following non-negotiable rules of fob access:

(a) One person, one key. My fob is personal and non-transferable. I will not share my fob, lend it, or allow anyone else to use it.
(b) No guests during fob hours. I will not bring any unauthorized person into the Facility, prop the door for others, or otherwise allow anyone to enter using my access. Door propping is grounds for immediate revocation of fob access without refund and may give rise to indemnification under §5.
(c) Video-only surveillance recording 24/7. I consent to being recorded by video-only surveillance cameras throughout the Facility at all times for security and rule enforcement. Cameras do not record audio. Notice of video surveillance is posted at the Facility entrance. Restrooms and any private changing area are not subject to video surveillance.
(d) Self-directed training only. No coaching, group instruction, or third-party training is permitted during fob hours.
(e) Personal training takes priority. If a coach is conducting a paid PT session and asks me to relocate, I will move immediately.
(f) Etiquette. I will wipe down equipment, return weights and equipment to their proper storage, and leave the Facility clean.
(g) No firearms, alcohol, or controlled substances on the premises (in compliance with the Illinois Firearm Concealed Carry Act and all applicable law). No-firearm signage is posted at the entrance.
(h) No biometric data collection at this time. Moonshot CrossFit does not currently collect, store, or use biometric identifiers (fingerprint, faceprint, voiceprint, hand or iris geometry) as defined under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, 740 ILCS 14. If Moonshot CrossFit adopts any biometric access control or surveillance technology in the future, members will be required to execute a separate BIPA-compliant written consent, with a published retention and destruction policy, before such system is enabled for them.
(i) No minors during fob hours. I will not bring any person under the age of 18 into the Facility during fob hours.
(j) Conduct. I will not engage in harassment, threats, discrimination, or other conduct that endangers or intimidates other members.
(k) Equipment damage. I am responsible for damage to Facility equipment caused by my misuse or abuse. Normal wear is not chargeable to me.
(l) Emergency procedures. I acknowledge that the AED location and emergency procedures are posted at the Facility, and that I will be shown the AED location before my fob is activated.
(m) No duty to monitor. Moonshot CrossFit has no duty to monitor surveillance cameras in real time or to dispatch help. The presence of cameras and other safety equipment does not create a special relationship or affirmative duty to rescue.
(n) Violation = revocation. Violation of any of the above results in immediate revocation of fob access with no refund of any prepaid amount, except as required by §9 and applicable Illinois law.

My membership term begins on the date my fob is first activated by Moonshot CrossFit's access-control system, not the date of payment. This is an auto-renewing subscription: annual plans renew yearly and monthly plans renew monthly at the rate I selected at signup, charged automatically to the payment method on file, until cancelled. No single prepaid period exceeds twelve (12) months, and this Agreement does not bind me to any term longer than thirty-six (36) months.

Cancellation. I may cancel at any time by emailing info@moonshotcrossfit.com before my next billing date. Cancellation stops future charges but, outside the refund triggers below, does not entitle me to a refund of amounts already paid.

Refund triggers (815 ILCS 645). In addition to the Three-Day Right of Cancellation at the top of this Agreement, I am entitled to cancel and receive a pro-rata refund of the unused portion of my current prepaid period upon: (a) my death (my estate may submit notice); (b) my permanent disability that prevents me from using the Facility for at least six (6) months, supported by physician documentation; or (c) my permanent relocation more than 25 miles from the Facility, supported by documentation.

Facility closure. If Moonshot CrossFit cancels the 24/7 fob program before my fob is activated, I receive a full refund. If Moonshot CrossFit discontinues the program after activation, I receive a refund of the unused portion of my current prepaid period.

Outside the triggers above, prepaid periods are non-refundable, except that Moonshot CrossFit may grant additional refunds in its sole discretion. My payment method is securely stored by Stripe; Moonshot CrossFit never sees or stores raw card details.

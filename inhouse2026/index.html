Workouts

Every athlete does their own level's reps, heights and loads no matter who is on their team. Harder option, fewer reps. Loads and heights are men / women.

01

Rig and Box

For time · relay, one athlete working · 2 rounds each, 6 total · 15:00 cap, plus 1 second per rep left at the cap

Each round is a pull, 10 box jump-overs, and a core movement. One athlete completes a full round at their level, then tags the next. One box per team, flipped to your height between athletes. No bands on the rig.

MovementFreedomIndependenceLiberty
Pull6 muscle-ups or 12 chest-to-bar8 chest-to-bar or 12 pull-ups or 16 jumping pull-ups6 pull-ups or 20 ring rows
Box10 box jump-overs, 30 / 24 in10 box jump-overs, 24 / 20 in10 step-overs, 16 / 12 in
Core15 toes-to-bar10 toes-to-bar or 20 knees-to-parallel20 hanging knee raises

Standards

  • Muscle-up: start from a full hang with arms straight; finish supported above the rings or bar with elbows locked out.
  • Chest-to-bar / pull-up: arms fully extended at the bottom. Chest touches the bar below the collarbone, or chin clears the bar. Kipping and butterfly are fine.
  • Jumping pull-up / ring row: jumping pull-ups finish with the chin over the bar. Ring rows start with arms straight and finish with the rings touching the chest, body in a straight line.
  • Box jump-over / step-over: jump-overs take off from both feet and both feet land on or over the box; you do not have to stand up tall on top. Step-overs may step up and down. Any direction, and you may touch the box with your hands.
  • Toes-to-bar / knees-to-parallel / knee raise: arms straight and heels behind the bar at the bottom of every rep. Toes-to-bar: both feet touch the bar together between the hands. Knees-to-parallel: thighs reach parallel or higher. Knee raise: knees come up to hip height.

Rules

  • One athlete works at a time. A round is all three movements, in order, by one athlete at their own level. Tag with a hand touch before the next athlete starts.
  • Pick your pull option before each round starts. You may choose a different option for your second round, never mid-set.
  • One box per team. Flipping it to the next athlete's height is on the clock.
  • No bands, no grips restrictions. Chalk is fine.
  • The judge counts out loud and calls no-reps; a no-rep is simply repeated. The head judge has the final call.
  • Score is your finish time. If you hit the 15:00 cap, score is 15:00 plus 1 second for every rep left.
02

Team Max Thruster

Max load · 9:00 window for the team · score is the sum of the three best lifts after multipliers

Standard

  • Bar starts on the floor. Squat clean straight into the thruster, or power clean, front squat, then thruster. Both count.
  • Hip crease below the knee at the bottom.
  • Bottom of the squat to lockout is one fluid motion. Press or push press, not a jerk. No pause or re-dip under the bar.
  • Finish with knees, hips and elbows locked out, bar over the middle of the foot.

Scoring

  • Two barbells per team, men's and women's. Any order, as many attempts as the window allows. Your judge follows the lifter.
  • Women's lifts are multiplied by 1.6.
  • Liberty athletes get an additional multiplier on top, so no team mix has an edge. The value is announced at the briefing.
  • Judges record the raw weight. Multipliers are applied at scoring.

Rules

  • Only one attempt counts at a time; a second athlete may be warming up, not attempting. Teammates load for each other.
  • A missed lift is a dump, not a bail. Clear the lane before the next attempt.
  • The final attempt must be locked out and called good before the 9:00 buzzer.
  • Collars on every attempt. Your judge records the raw weight of your best good lift.
03

Dumbbells Only

For time · relay, one athlete working · 3 rounds each, 9 total · 15:00 cap, plus 1 second per rep left at the cap

Each round: 8 shoulder-to-overhead, 8 front squats, 8 burpees over the dumbbells, set side by side. Same reps at every level, only the load changes. Each athlete owns one pair of dumbbells for the whole event. Tag in any order and change the order between rounds.

Two dumbbellsFreedomIndependenceLiberty
Men / women50 / 35 lb40 / 30 lb25 / 20 lb

Standards

  • Shoulder-to-overhead: dumbbells start on the shoulders and finish locked out overhead, hips, knees and elbows extended, dumbbells over the middle of the body. Strict press, push press or jerk all count.
  • Front squat: dumbbells rest on the shoulders, hip crease passes below the knee at the bottom, stand to full hip and knee extension. No squat cleans: the dumbbells start on the shoulders.
  • Burpee over dumbbells: chest and thighs touch the floor, then jump or step over the dumbbells with both feet landing on the other side. Any style, either side, no need to stand tall. Do not touch the dumbbells.

Rules

  • One athlete works at a time. A round is 8 shoulder-to-overhead, 8 front squats, 8 burpees over, in that order. Tag with a hand touch.
  • Each athlete owns one pair of dumbbells at their level for the whole event and never lifts another pair.
  • Dumbbells are set side by side on the floor for the burpees. If they get kicked apart, reset them before the next burpee.
  • Any athlete order, change it between rounds, but every athlete finishes three rounds before the team is done.
  • Score is your finish time. If you hit the 15:00 cap, score is 15:00 plus 1 second for every rep left.

Floaters

Two extra scored events your team does on its own time, any time between 8:00 and 11:05. Sign up at the floater board when you arrive. All three athletes work at once.

Bermuda Triangle (Get Lost)

Row · Ski · Bike · total calories

Each athlete starts on a different machine. 1:00 max calories, 15 seconds to rotate, 1:00, 15 seconds, 1:00, so every athlete hits all three machines. Rotation is row to ski to bike to row. Score is the team's total calories. No levels, everyone does the same thing.

Get Yoked

Sandbag over a 4 ft yoke · total reps

Each athlete gets 1:00 to get a sandbag over the yoke bar as many times as possible. Team total.

SandbagFreedomIndependenceLiberty
Men / women100 / 80 lb80 / 60 lb60 / 40 lb

Standards

  • Bermuda Triangle: calories as the monitor shows them. Hands off the handle, paddle or pedals until "go" and at "time". Monitors are reset before each team starts; the judge reads them at the end of each minute. Any damper or resistance setting.
  • Get Yoked: the bag must pass fully over the 4 ft yoke bar and land on the other side. Any method: shoulder, bear hug, throw. The bag may touch the bar on the way over. Walk around the yoke to retrieve it; the clock keeps running.

Rules

  • Sign up at the floater board when you arrive. Both floaters close when Event 3 starts at 11:05, so get them done early.
  • Bermuda Triangle: each athlete starts on a different machine. 1:00 work, 0:15 to move to the next machine, until every athlete has done row, ski and bike. Everyone uses the same bike.
  • Get Yoked: one athlete at a time, 1:00 each, team total. Bag weights are set by your level (men / women): Freedom 100 / 80, Independence 80 / 60, Liberty 60 / 40.
  • Floater scores count the same as the three events. A no-rep is repeated, head judge has the final call.

Scoring

Like golf: lowest score wins. Five scored pieces, the three events and both floaters, all worth the same.

Points per event

Teams are ranked in every scored piece. 1st place gets 1 point, 2nd gets 2, and so on down to 15. Teams that tie share the better place, and the next team takes the place after the tie.

Overall

Add up your points from all five pieces. The lowest total wins. A perfect day is 5 points.

Tiebreakers, in order

  1. Most first-place finishes.
  2. Most total calories on Bermuda Triangle.
  3. Most total reps on Get Yoked.

How it works

Teams of three

15 teams, balanced from Open and Wodify results and the coaches' reads. 4 lanes: heats 2 to 4 run four teams, heat 1 runs three. Your team stays in the same heat and lane all day.

Freedom, Independence, Liberty

Every athlete is placed in one of three levels by the coaches: Freedom is advanced, Independence is intermediate, Liberty is scaled. You do your level's version of every movement no matter who is on your team.

One athlete working

Events 1 and 3 are relays. One athlete works, two rest, tag to switch. Any order, change it between rounds. One judge per team. Spectators welcome.

Loads and heights are men / women. Everyone does their own level's reps no matter who is on their team.

Rules

  • One athlete works at a time. A round is all three movements, in order, at your own level. Tag with a hand touch.
  • Pick your pull option before each round starts. You may change it for round two, never mid-set.
  • One box per team. Flipping it to the next height is on the clock.
  • No bands on the rig. Chalk is fine.
  • Box jump-overs: two-foot take-off, both feet land on or over the box. No need to stand tall. Hands on the box are fine.
  • Toes-to-bar: heels behind the bar at the bottom, both feet touch the bar together.
  • The judge counts out loud. A no-rep is repeated. Head judge has the final call.
Event02

Team Max ThrusterMax load · two barbells per team, men's and women's

9:00 windowScore is the sum of the three best lifts after multipliers

Floor to overhead
squat clean thruster, or power clean + front squat + thruster

MultiplierFreedomIndependenceLiberty
Men× 1.0× 1.0× 1.0 + Liberty bonus
Women× 1.6× 1.6× 1.6 + Liberty bonus
Judges record the raw weight of your best good lift. Multipliers are applied at scoring. The Liberty bonus is announced at the briefing.

Standard

  • Bar starts on the floor.
  • Hip crease below the knee at the bottom.
  • Bottom of the squat to lockout in one fluid motion. Press or push press, not a jerk. No pause or re-dip under the bar.
  • Finish with knees, hips and elbows locked out, bar over the middle of the foot.

Rules

  • One attempt at a time. A second athlete may warm up, not attempt. Teammates load for each other.
  • Collars on every attempt. A missed lift is a dump, not a bail. Clear the lane before the next attempt.
  • The last attempt must be locked out and called good before the 9:00 buzzer.

Two dumbbellsFreedomIndependenceLiberty
Men / women50 / 35 lb40 / 30 lb25 / 20 lb
Each athlete owns one pair of dumbbells for the whole event. Tag in any order and change the order between rounds.

Standards

  • Shoulder-to-overhead: from the shoulders to locked out overhead, hips, knees and elbows extended. Strict, push press or jerk all count.
  • Front squat: dumbbells on the shoulders, hip crease below the knee, stand to full extension. No squat cleans.
  • Burpee over: chest and thighs touch the floor, then jump or step over both dumbbells. Any style, no need to stand tall. Do not touch the dumbbells.

Rules

  • Dumbbells sit side by side for the burpees. Kicked apart means reset before the next rep.
  • Every athlete finishes three rounds before the team is done.
  • Hit the cap and your score is 15:00 plus 1 second per rep left.

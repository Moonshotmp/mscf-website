A Whole-Person Approach

This isn't a meal plan handed to you and left at that. My coaching is built on a whole-person philosophy — the same evidence-based, behavior-first approach used by Precision Nutrition — which means we look beyond calories and macros to the full picture of your life: your schedule, your stress, your sleep, your relationship with food, and the habits that actually fit into your day-to-day.

Real, lasting change doesn't come from a rigid plan you push through for a few weeks. It comes from small, sustainable shifts, built one at a time, with someone in your corner who adjusts the plan as your life changes — not the other way around. That's the role I play: your coach, your accountability partner, and your guide toward a healthier, more sustainable way of living.

What Every Client Gets

  • ✓A personalized strategy built around your goals, schedule, and preferences.
  • ✓Ongoing meal planning guidance that changes as your life does.
  • ✓Exercise and movement recommendations that complement your nutrition goals.
  • ✓A collaborative, judgment-free space to talk through wins, setbacks, and everything in between.
  • ✓Regular check-ins to track progress and refine the plan together.
